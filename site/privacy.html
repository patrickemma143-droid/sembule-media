Who we are

Sembule Media is a live production company based in Bunamwaya, Kampala, working across Uganda. We run livestreams, Zoom and hybrid conferences, sound and PA, documentaries, photography and film. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use this website or work with us.

What we collect

  • What you send us: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, organisation, and details about your event, such as the date, venue, audience size and budget.
  • What we need to deliver a production: contacts for the venue and your team, programme details, and the platforms and links for a livestream.
  • What this website records: the site does not set analytics or advertising cookies at present. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare a quote, plan and deliver your production, send invoices and receipts, and follow up afterwards. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Why we are allowed to

We handle personal information in line with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. We rely on your consent when you contact us, on the need to perform an agreement once you book, and on legal and accounting duties for financial records.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with the crew and trusted freelancers working on your production, with payment and banking providers, and with the platforms you ask us to stream to, such as YouTube, Facebook or Zoom. We may also share information where the law requires it.

People in our footage and photos

Events we cover are filmed and photographed, and people attending may appear in the stream, recording or pictures. If you would like an image of yourself removed from our website or social channels, contact us and we will take it down. For private events, letting guests know that the event is being filmed or streamed is agreed with the organiser.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as we need it to deliver and follow up on your production, and financial records for as long as the law requires. When we no longer need information, we delete it or store it securely.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, and object to how we use it. Contact us using the details below and we will reply as soon as we can.

Other services this site uses

Some links and features on this website are run by other companies, each with its own terms and privacy notice: WhatsApp (our enquiry form opens a message there for you to send), YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google Maps.
