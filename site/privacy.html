How this website handles information when you contact Sembule Media.

Draft for owner review. Confirm the final contact, retention, analytics and enquiry-handling details before publishing this notice.

Information you choose to send

The project enquiry form prepares a message in WhatsApp for you to review and send. The draft form does not store the details you enter on this website. If you choose to send the message, WhatsApp processes it under its own terms and privacy notice.

Email and direct contact

If you contact Sembule Media by email, phone or another channel, the information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the production. Confirm the business retention practice and contact for privacy questions before launch.

Cookies and analytics

This draft site does not load analytics until the owner supplies and configures an approved GA4 measurement ID. If analytics are enabled, update this notice with the relevant cookie and measurement details before publishing.

Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp, YouTube and the Sembule Media workspace open services hosted outside this website. Their own terms and privacy notices apply when you use them.

Contact

For a question about this draft notice, email sembule@sembulemedia.com. Confirm this address and final privacy contact with Sembule Media before launch.
