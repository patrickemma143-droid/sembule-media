About these terms

These terms apply when you use this website and when you book Sembule Media for a production. The quote or agreement for your event sets out the specific scope, price and dates; if anything in it differs from these terms, the quote or agreement applies.

Quotes and bookings

  • Sending an enquiry does not reserve a date.
  • A quote lists the services, crew, equipment, deliverables and price for your production, and how long the quote stays valid.
  • Your booking is confirmed once you accept the quote in writing and pay the deposit stated in it. Until then, the date stays open to other clients.

Payment

Payments are due as set out in your quote and invoice, in Uganda Shillings unless agreed otherwise. If the balance is not paid when due, we may hold back the final deliverables until it is.

Bookings & cancellation

  • If you need to cancel: tell us in writing as early as possible. Deposits and any cancellation charges are applied as set out in your quote, because a confirmed date means we turn other work away.
  • If you need to change the date: we will move your booking to a new date where crew and equipment are free. Any difference in cost is agreed before the change.
  • If the scope changes: extra cameras, hours, locations or deliverables are quoted and agreed before the event.
  • If we cannot attend: in the rare case that we must cancel, we will arrange equivalent crew or equipment where we can. If we cannot, we refund what you have paid for the services we did not deliver.

What we need from you

  • Access to the venue in good time to set up and test, and a safe place for our equipment.
  • Stable power near the production desk.
  • Permission from the venue to film, stream and run cables.
  • The programme, speaker list and any slides or videos ahead of the day.
  • For livestreams: the platform accounts or links, and access rights where we stream to your channel.

Livestreams

Live streaming depends on mobile and internet networks and on platforms such as YouTube, Facebook and Zoom, which are outside our control. We test the stream before going live and bring connections on more than one network to reduce the risk of a drop. We are not responsible for outages, interruptions or decisions made by those networks and platforms.

Footage, photos and their use

  • You receive the deliverables listed in your quote for your own use, including sharing with your guests, members and partners.
  • Sembule Media keeps the right to show selected footage and photos from your event in our portfolio and on our social channels. If you would rather we did not, tell us in writing before the event.
  • Music and third-party content you ask us to include remain your responsibility to licence.

Liability

We take care with every production. Our total responsibility for any claim connected with a booking is limited to the amount you paid for that booking. We are not responsible for indirect losses, such as lost revenue, or for events outside our reasonable control, such as power cuts, severe weather or venue restrictions.

Using this website

The text, photographs, video and brand assets on this website belong to Sembule Media or are used with permission. Please do not copy or reuse them without asking. Links to other websites are provided for convenience; we do not control their content or terms.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Uganda. If a disagreement arises, we will first try to settle it directly with you.
